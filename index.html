About the gym

More than a gym.
A camp.

ALEXY Team is a Muay Thai, K1 and kickboxing gym in Nicosia, built on hard work and real coaching — taught in a way that works for real people with real schedules.

Run by coaches Alexy and Levo, the team trains everyone from kids taking their first class to fighters walking out on King of Kings (KOK) fight nights. Whether you've never thrown a kick or you're preparing for the ring, you'll get hands-on coaching. Every class blends technique, padwork, conditioning and controlled sparring so you progress safely and fast.

No excuses. No shortcuts.
Just discipline, sweat and getting stronger.

FAQ

Before your first class

Still unsure? Message us — we're happy to help you get started.

I've never trained before. Can I join?

Absolutely — most of our members started from zero. The Mix Adults Group welcomes complete beginners and you'll never be thrown into sparring before you're ready.

What should I bring?

Comfortable sportswear, water and a towel. We have gloves and hand wraps you can borrow for your first sessions. Training is barefoot.

Do I have to spar?

No. Sparring is optional and always controlled. Many members train purely for fitness and technique.

Do you have classes for kids?

Yes — Kids 5–10 at 16:00 and Kids 10–15 at 17:00, Monday to Friday. Younger kids focus on basics, drills and circuit training.

Am I fit enough?

You'll get fit by training — not before. Coaches scale every drill to your level.
